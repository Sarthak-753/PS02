## 🚨 About RESQ

**RESQ Intelligence — AURA 1.0** is an AI-assisted disaster command and proactive resilience platform designed to support emergency response teams during complex disaster scenarios.

The platform combines **AI-driven risk intelligence, tactical mapping, resource allocation, hospital coordination, route intelligence, simulation, and resilience analysis** into a unified operational environment.

RESQ is designed around a complete disaster-response workflow:

> **Predict → Prepare → Position → Respond → Recover**

The objective is to help decision-makers understand rapidly changing situations, compare response strategies, allocate critical resources, and identify potential cascading failures before they become operational crises.

# 🧠 AURA Intelligence

**AURA** is the intelligence layer of RESQ.

It analyzes operational conditions and provides decision-support information that can assist command teams in understanding:

- Disaster risk
- Threat severity
- Resource requirements
- Emergency response priorities
- Route conditions
- Hospital capacity
- Critical resource availability
- Potential cascade failures
- Operational resilience

AURA is designed to transform complex disaster information into actionable operational intelligence.

# 🛰️ Core Platform Capabilities

## Command Center

Provides a centralized operational view of an ongoing disaster scenario.

Command teams can monitor incidents, resources, response activities, and operational priorities from a unified interface.

---

## 🗺️ Tactical Disaster Map

Provides a visual representation of the disaster environment, including operational areas, incidents, routes, resources, and critical locations.

The map supports situational awareness and tactical decision-making.

---

## 🚑 Emergency Resource Dispatch

Supports the allocation and positioning of emergency resources such as:

- Ambulances
- Emergency teams
- Rescue resources
- Critical supplies
- Medical resources

The objective is to position resources where they can provide the greatest operational value.

---

## 🏥 Hospital Intelligence

Helps monitor hospital-related operational conditions and supports decision-making around:

- Hospital availability
- Patient capacity
- Emergency demand
- Medical resource requirements
- Resource distribution

---

## 🫁 Critical Resource Management

Supports monitoring and allocation of critical emergency resources such as oxygen and other medical supplies.

RESQ focuses on identifying resource shortages and improving distribution decisions during high-demand situations.

# 📊 Response Strategy Comparison

RESQ supports multiple response strategies so command teams can compare different operational decisions.

### Plan A — Fastest

Prioritizes rapid response and minimum response time.

### Plan B — Resilient

Balances response speed with route redundancy, reserve resources, and operational resilience.

### Plan C — Maximum Coverage

Prioritizes broader operational coverage and resource availability across the affected region.

This allows decision-makers to evaluate the trade-offs between **speed, coverage, and resilience** rather than relying on a single response plan.

---

# 🔮 Proactive Resilience

RESQ is not limited to responding to an existing disaster.

The platform also focuses on understanding what could happen next.

### What-If Simulation

Allows operational scenarios to be explored before decisions are implemented.

### Cascade Failure Analysis

Helps identify how the failure or shortage of one component could affect other parts of the disaster-response system.

### Resource Gravity

Analyzes how critical resources influence operational response and where resources may have the greatest impact.

### Digital Twin

Provides a simulated representation of the operational environment for analysis and scenario evaluation.

### Resilience Stress Test

Tests the response system under challenging conditions to identify weaknesses and potential failure points.
